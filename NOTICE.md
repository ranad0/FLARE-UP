# Sources, assets and development acknowledgements

We built FLARE UP as an independent NASA Space Apps project. It is not an official NASA product, and NASA names do not imply endorsement.

## Scientific sources
NASA PSI records, reports and supporting NASA webpages retain their original attribution and embedded notices. We preserve source URLs, DOI where available, filenames, page/row locations and hashes. We distinguish experimental evidence from research objectives, context and unreviewed material.

## Application and visual assets
Our application combines our team's data pipeline and earlier frontend/backend prototypes. We used AI-assisted coding and design tools during development. The circular flame mark is based on our supplied brand reference; its transparent version was prepared using an image-editing tool. The interactive flames and smoke are illustrative artwork, not calibrated physical predictions.
